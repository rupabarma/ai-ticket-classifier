About TicketFlow

A small, readable example of machine learning routing customer support tickets.

What TicketFlow does

You enter a ticket subject and description. TicketFlow predicts which team should handle it (Billing, Technical, HR or General), says how confident it is, and marks the ticket Urgent or Normal. If the model is not sure enough, it asks for a person to decide.

How the model works

The text is cleaned, then TF-IDF turns it into numbers. Words and two-word phrases that are rare overall but common in a department, such as "refund" or "reset link", get more weight. A Logistic Regression classifier learns which weighted phrases point to which department and outputs a probability for each one.

The highest probability is the confidence. At or above {{ threshold_pct }}% the department is assigned. Below it, the ticket is marked Needs Human Review.

Classification workflow

  1. SubmitYou send a subject and description through the form.
  2. CleanSubject and body are joined, lowercased, and stripped of links and symbols.
  3. VectorizeThe saved TF-IDF vectorizer turns the text into features.
  4. PredictThe classifier returns a probability for each department.
  5. DecideAt least {{ threshold_pct }}% confidence is auto-classified. Less goes to human review.
  6. PrioritizeKeyword rules mark the ticket Urgent or Normal.
